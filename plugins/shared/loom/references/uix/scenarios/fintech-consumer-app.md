# Fintech Consumer App UIX

Use for customer-facing banking, wallet, payment, trading, insurance, or wealth experiences.

## Baseline

- Build for trust, clarity, and recovery. Avoid flashy finance cliches that obscure risk or precision.
- Primary balances, holdings, payments, trades, and confirmations need readable hierarchy and explicit units.
- Risky operations require confirmation, authentication or verification hooks when in scope, and clear receipts.

## Required Patterns

- Mobile-first flows need reachable primary actions, safe-area handling, keyboard-aware inputs, and touch-friendly controls.
- Money inputs need formatting, validation, limits, and error recovery.
- Transaction and order histories need status, timestamp, amount, counterparty or instrument, and drill-down when in scope.
