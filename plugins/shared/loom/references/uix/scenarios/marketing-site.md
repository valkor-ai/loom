# Marketing Site UIX

Use for landing pages, product marketing, conversion pages, launches, and campaign pages.

## Baseline

- The first viewport must communicate the actual offer, product, or brand, not a generic decorative layout.
- Use real product, place, object, workflow, or generated bitmap imagery when imagery is needed.
- Keep claims specific and support the primary conversion path.

## Required Patterns

- Hero, proof, feature, pricing, FAQ, and CTA sections should serve a clear decision path.
- Avoid generic hero plus feature-card templates that could fit any product.
- Mobile layout must keep headline, CTA, and next content visible without overlap.
